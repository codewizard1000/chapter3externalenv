Product Life Cycle: From innovation to global production

Vernon’s framework predicts that production and trade patterns can shift as a product moves from new to maturing to standardized.

1 · New product

Production stays close to sophisticated demand, engineers, and early users; uncertainty is high.

2 · Maturing product

Demand grows abroad, exports rise, techniques become more standardized, and foreign production may begin.

3 · Standardized product

Price competition intensifies; production often shifts toward lower-cost locations and the innovating country can become an importer.
